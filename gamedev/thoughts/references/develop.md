# Develop

Grow a thought card toward ripe. The user supplies the taste; you supply the structure.

## Procedure

1. Read the card, plus any linked cards.
2. Restate the idea in one sentence to confirm understanding.
3. Ask sharp questions, one at a time. Good questions:
   - "What should the player *feel* in that moment?"
   - "What's the smallest version a player could actually touch?"
   - "What existing system does this lean on / fight with?"
4. Connect: search `thoughts/` and `experiments/graveyard/` for relatives. Surface them: "T-007 was similar — it died because: <cause>." A graveyard hit is valuable information, not a reason to drop the idea.
5. Slice: propose the smallest testable unit — often a single parameter or interaction, not the whole feature.
6. As answers arrive, fill Hypothesis / Slice / Blockers / Notes on the card and bump `status` (seed → sprouting → ripe) and `updated`.
7. When the card is ripe, say so and ask: "Want to prototype it?" Then wait.

## Forbidden

- Editing the original quote line
- Auto-promoting to prototype (that is the user's call — human gate)
- Writing spec-length documents; the card stays short
- Judging the idea's worth; you only judge testability
